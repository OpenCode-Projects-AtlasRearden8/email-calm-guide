Quick request that should be easy to either grant or turn down:

**__What you need, in one short sentence.__**

Context: __one line of why, only as much as it helps. If it's nothing, write "No context needed — you've got the full picture already."__

By when: __date / "whenever suits this month"__. And if the answer is no or not-now, a one-line reply is a complete answer and much appreciated.

Thanks!
